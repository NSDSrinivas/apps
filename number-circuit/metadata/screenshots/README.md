# Product-page images

The product page uses the six current App Store marketing PNGs per device from
`NumberCruncher/AppStore/1.1/draft 5/iPhone` and `iPad`. Draft 5 was prepared
from fresh Number Spark 1.1 (build 5) simulator captures and replaces the older
draft-2 gallery. Source provenance and checksums are recorded in draft 5's
`README.md` and `validation.json`.

Order: Make It Yours, A Challenge Every Day, Make Mental Math Fun, Chase Your
Best, Find Your Challenge, Build Your Daily Streak.

The `iphone/app-store` and `ipad/app-store` directories hold the validated final
exports without modification. Their parent device directories hold the matching
current raw captures; the hub uses the iPhone Home and gameplay captures. Older
root-level images are historical assets.
