# Privacy details

- **Does the app collect any data at all?** No — Number Circuit does not collect, store, or transmit personal data for developer use.
- **Information collected:** None by the developer. Scores, best runs, Daily Challenge history, and settings are stored locally on-device using Apple's standard on-device storage (`UserDefaults`). The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- **Why each item is collected:** N/A — nothing is collected by the developer.
- **Third-party services used:** Game Center (Apple) is optional. If a player chooses to use it, authentication, leaderboard scores, and related service data are processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer does not receive the player's Apple ID credentials or any Game Center data.
- **Data retention & deletion:** Deleting the app deletes all data stored locally by the app. Apple may separately retain Game Center service data according to its own policies and the player's Game Center settings.
- **Directed at children?** No — not specifically directed at children under 13.
- **Account required to use the app?** No. Game Center sign-in is optional and only needed to submit scores to leaderboards; the game is fully playable without it.
