# FAQ

- **Q: Do I need an account to play?**
  A: No. Number Circuit works fully offline with no sign-up. Game Center is optional and only needed if you want to submit scores to leaderboards.
- **Q: Does Number Circuit collect my data or show ads?**
  A: No. There are no ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Scores, best runs, and Daily Challenge history are stored locally on your device.
- **Q: What happens if I get an answer wrong?**
  A: You lose one of three hearts and see the correct answer, then the run continues — a mistake doesn't end things immediately.
- **Q: What's the difference between Sprint and Survival?**
  A: Sprint is a fixed 10-question run against the clock. Survival has no finish line, and the timer gets a little tighter with every question.
- **Q: What is the Daily Challenge?**
  A: One personalized challenge per day, built from your own recent play history. Your Daily Streak and history are tracked on the Streak tab.
