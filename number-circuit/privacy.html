Privacy Policy for Math Puzzles

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

What this covers

Math Puzzles is displayed as Number Spark on your device. This policy explains what information Math Puzzles collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information we collect

None. Math Puzzles does not collect, store, or transmit personal data for developer use. Scores, best runs, Daily Challenge history, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

Not applicable — nothing is collected by the developer.

Third parties

Game Center is optional. If you choose to use it, authentication, leaderboard scores, and related service data are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Math Puzzles does not receive your Apple ID credentials.

Data retention & deletion

Deleting Math Puzzles deletes the data stored locally by the app. Apple may separately retain Game Center service data according to its own policies and your Game Center settings.

Children

Math Puzzles is not specifically directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the Support page.
