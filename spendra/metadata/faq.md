# FAQ

- **Q: Is Spendra free?**
  A: Yes. Spendra is free to use with no sign-in required — unlimited manual expense tracking, your dashboard, an overall monthly budget, versioned JSON backup and restore, and full access to delete your own data. An optional Spendra Pro upgrade (monthly, annual, or a one-time lifetime purchase) unlocks unlimited receipt scanning, full Insights, recurring automation, CSV export, and quick add with Siri and App Intents, from the Mac menu bar, and on Apple Watch. If Pro ever lapses, none of your existing data is deleted, hidden or locked.
- **Q: Do I need to create an account?**
  A: No. Spendra has no sign-in or account requirement — sync relies on your existing iCloud account.
- **Q: How does Spendra sync across my devices?**
  A: Records are stored in a private iCloud database and synchronised automatically across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Preferences and settings stay device-local.
- **Q: Does Spendra connect to my bank?**
  A: No. Spendra does not connect to bank accounts. You enter expenses manually or by scanning a receipt.
- **Q: Can I track expenses in multiple currencies?**
  A: Yes. Spendra supports the full ISO currency list. Each currency is reported separately — Spendra does not convert between currencies or provide exchange rates.
- **Q: How does receipt scanning work?**
  A: Choose a receipt image from your photo library on iPhone, iPad or Mac, or take a photo on a supported iPhone or iPad. Camera permission is needed only when taking a photo. Apple Vision processes the image on-device and suggests details you can review before saving. Three useful scans are complimentary; Spendra Pro removes the limit.
- **Q: Can I use Spendra on Apple Watch?**
  A: Yes, with Spendra Pro. A quick-add complication and app on Apple Watch let you log an expense from your wrist. Entries queue for delivery to your paired iPhone through WatchConnectivity and then sync through iCloud. Delivery can wait until connectivity is available; entries remain pending until the iPhone confirms they were saved.
- **Q: Can I automate recurring expenses?**
  A: Yes, with Spendra Pro. It unlocks recurring-expense automation, subscription detection, price-change insights and renewal reminders. Viewing, editing and deleting existing data remains available if Pro lapses. Spendra currently supports one overall monthly budget rather than per-category budgets.
- **Q: Can I export my data?**
  A: Yes. JSON backup is free on every plan, any time from Settings. CSV export is part of Spendra Pro.
- **Q: How do I delete my data?**
  A: You can delete individual records from within the app, or reset all records from Settings — this propagates the deletion across your synced devices.
- **Q: Can I lock the app for privacy?**
  A: Yes. Spendra supports a Face ID (or other device authentication) privacy lock that you can enable in Settings.
