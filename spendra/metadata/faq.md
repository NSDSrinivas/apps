# FAQ

- **Q: Is Spendra free?**
  A: Yes. Spendra is free to use with no sign-in required — unlimited manual expense tracking, your dashboard, a basic budget, and full access to export or delete your own data, always. An optional Spendra Pro upgrade (monthly, annual, or a one-time lifetime purchase) unlocks unlimited receipt scanning, full Insights, recurring automation, templates, and quick add from Siri, Apple Watch and the Mac menu bar. If Pro ever lapses, none of your existing data is deleted, hidden or locked.
- **Q: Do I need to create an account?**
  A: No. Spendra has no sign-in or account requirement — sync relies on your existing iCloud account.
- **Q: How does Spendra sync across my devices?**
  A: Records are stored in a private iCloud database and synchronised automatically across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Preferences and settings stay device-local.
- **Q: Does Spendra connect to my bank?**
  A: No. Spendra does not connect to bank accounts. You enter expenses manually or by scanning a receipt.
- **Q: Can I track expenses in multiple currencies?**
  A: Yes. Spendra supports the full ISO currency list. Each currency is reported separately — Spendra does not convert between currencies or provide exchange rates.
- **Q: How does receipt scanning work?**
  A: With the camera permission enabled, Spendra uses Apple's Vision framework to extract details from a photographed receipt so you can review and save them as an expense. Every account gets three complimentary scans; Spendra Pro removes the limit.
- **Q: Can I use Spendra on Apple Watch?**
  A: Yes, with Spendra Pro. A quick-add complication and app on Apple Watch let you log an expense from your wrist. Entries transfer to your paired iPhone through WatchConnectivity and then sync; a paired connection is required.
- **Q: Can I set up recurring expenses, templates or category budgets?**
  A: Viewing, editing and deleting anything you've already saved is always free. Creating new recurring rules or templates, and Spendra's automatic posting and subscription-price-change detection, are part of Spendra Pro. The overall monthly budget is free; per-category budgets are on the roadmap.
- **Q: Can I export my data?**
  A: Yes. JSON backup is free on every plan, any time from Settings. CSV export is part of Spendra Pro.
- **Q: How do I delete my data?**
  A: You can delete individual records from within the app, or reset all records from Settings — this propagates the deletion across your synced devices.
- **Q: Can I lock the app for privacy?**
  A: Yes. Spendra supports a Face ID (or other device authentication) privacy lock that you can enable in Settings.
