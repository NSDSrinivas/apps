# App Store description

- **Promotional text** (170 chars, editable without review): Track expenses, plan budgets and understand spending across iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch — with private iCloud sync across devices.
- **Description:**

Make everyday spending easier to see and easier to plan. Spendra is a focused expense tracker for recording purchases, setting budgets and understanding where your money goes — across iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Free, always:

- Unlimited manual expense creation, editing, duplication, deletion, history and search.
- The current-month dashboard, category breakdown, and a basic monthly budget.
- Default accounts, categories, currency, theme and reminders.
- Face ID privacy lock and app-switcher privacy protection.
- JSON backup and full access to export or delete your own records, any time.
- Three complimentary receipt scans per install.

Spendra Pro unlocks:

- Unlimited receipt scanning with Apple Vision.
- Full Insights — weekly, monthly and yearly periods, comparisons, charts and category drill-down.
- Recurring expenses with automatic posting, subscription detection, price-change alerts and renewal reminders.
- Expense templates for faster entry.
- Quick add from Siri, the Action Button, the Mac menu bar and Apple Watch.
- CSV export (JSON backup stays free on every plan).

Spendra Pro is available as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase — pricing is shown in the app before you buy, and Restore Purchases is available from the paywall and Settings. Downgrading or letting a subscription lapse never deletes, hides or locks your existing data — everything you've already entered stays fully visible, editable and exportable.

Spendra syncs records through a private iCloud database. Apple Watch entries transfer to the paired iPhone through WatchConnectivity and then sync; availability depends on pairing and connection. Preferences remain device-local.

Choose from the full ISO currency list. Spendra reports each currency separately and does not convert between currencies or provide exchange rates.

Spendra has no sign-in requirement. It does not connect to banks or claim to provide financial advice.
