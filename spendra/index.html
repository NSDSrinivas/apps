About

Make everyday spending easier to see and easier to plan. Spendra is a focused expense tracker for recording purchases, setting budgets and understanding where your money goes — across iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Free, always: unlimited manual expense creation, editing, duplication, deletion, history and search; the current-month dashboard and category breakdown; a basic monthly budget; default accounts, categories, currency, theme and reminders; Face ID privacy lock; and full access to export a JSON backup or delete your own records, any time.

  • Add and organise expenses with categories, accounts, tags and notes.
  • See a clear dashboard and searchable history with filters for the details that matter.
  • Set an overall monthly budget, with reminders to help you stay on track.
  • Export your records as CSV or JSON, and browse spending through calendar history.
  • Use Face ID privacy lock and manage reminders, appearance and local preferences from Settings.

Spendra Pro is an optional upgrade — monthly, annual, or a one-time lifetime purchase — that unlocks:

  • Unlimited receipt scanning with Apple Vision (three scans are free on every plan).
  • Full Insights — weekly, monthly and yearly periods, comparisons, charts and category drill-down.
  • Recurring expenses with automatic posting, subscription detection, price-change alerts and renewal reminders.
  • Expense templates for faster, more consistent entry.
  • Quick add from Siri, the Action Button, the Mac menu bar and Apple Watch.

Pricing is shown in the app before you buy, and Restore Purchases is available from the paywall and Settings. If Spendra Pro ever lapses, your existing data is never deleted, hidden or locked — you keep full access to everything you've already recorded.

Spendra syncs records through a private iCloud database. Apple Watch entries transfer to the paired iPhone through WatchConnectivity and then sync; availability depends on pairing and connection. Preferences remain device-local.

Choose from the full ISO currency list. Spendra reports each currency separately and does not convert between currencies or provide exchange rates. Spendra has no sign-in requirement and does not connect to banks or claim to provide financial advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is Spendra free?
Yes. Spendra is free to use with no sign-in required — unlimited manual expense tracking, your dashboard, a basic budget, and full access to export or delete your own data, always. An optional Spendra Pro upgrade (monthly, annual, or a one-time lifetime purchase) unlocks unlimited receipt scanning, full Insights, recurring automation, templates, and quick add from Siri, Apple Watch and the Mac menu bar. If Pro ever lapses, none of your existing data is deleted, hidden or locked.

Do I need to create an account?
No. Spendra has no sign-in or account requirement — sync relies on your existing iCloud account.

How does Spendra sync across my devices?
Records are stored in a private iCloud database and synchronised automatically across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Preferences and settings stay device-local.

Does Spendra connect to my bank?
No. Spendra does not connect to bank accounts. You enter expenses manually or by scanning a receipt.

Can I track expenses in multiple currencies?
Yes. Spendra supports the full ISO currency list. Each currency is reported separately — Spendra does not convert between currencies or provide exchange rates.

How does receipt scanning work?
With the camera permission enabled, Spendra uses Apple's Vision framework to extract details from a photographed receipt so you can review and save them as an expense. Every account gets three complimentary scans; Spendra Pro removes the limit.

Can I use Spendra on Apple Watch?
Yes, with Spendra Pro. A quick-add complication and app on Apple Watch let you log an expense from your wrist. Entries transfer to your paired iPhone through WatchConnectivity and then sync; a paired connection is required.

Can I set up recurring expenses, templates or category budgets?
Viewing, editing and deleting anything you've already saved is always free. Creating new recurring rules or templates, and Spendra's automatic posting and subscription-price-change detection, are part of Spendra Pro. The overall monthly budget is free; per-category budgets are on the roadmap.

Can I export my data?
Yes. JSON backup is free on every plan, any time from Settings. CSV export is part of Spendra Pro.

How do I delete my data?
You can delete individual records from within the app, or reset all records from Settings — this propagates the deletion across your synced devices. This is always available, regardless of plan.

Can I lock the app for privacy?
Yes. Spendra supports a Face ID (or other device authentication) privacy lock that you can enable in Settings.
